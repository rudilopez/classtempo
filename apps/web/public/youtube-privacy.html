YouTube privacy and playback

Updated October 29, 2026.

YouTube is an optional video source. Paste a video link and choose Open video to load YouTube's official privacy-enhanced embedded player. A video opens paused; use Play when ready.

When ClassTempo connects to YouTube

  • Opening the ordinary ClassTempo player or the empty YouTube link form does not load the YouTube player. Opening a valid video loads YouTube's player and its services.
  • The embedded player shares information with YouTube to display the video, check availability, and operate playback. YouTube and Google may collect device, network, viewing and cookie information under their own policies.
  • ClassTempo does not ask for a YouTube password or request access to your YouTube account, channel, subscriptions or private library. YouTube may ask you to sign in on its own pages for restricted content.

What stays in this browser

  • Saved cue positions and names are stored in this browser's local storage, separately for each YouTube video. They are not included in ClassTempo's local-file settings backup.
  • The video link, title and player state are used to display and control your current video. ClassTempo does not download, record, extract, separate or save YouTube audio or video for offline use.
  • Switching sources or leaving the app pauses YouTube. Stage enlarges the same embedded player; it keeps YouTube's own controls, branding and advertisements available.

Availability and controls

Internet is required. The video owner, YouTube, your location and account restrictions determine whether a video can be embedded. Available playback speeds come from YouTube and may vary by video or device. Some links must be opened directly on YouTube. Practice, class plans, stems and offline playback use imported files.

Read Google's privacy policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.
