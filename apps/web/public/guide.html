    01 · THE BASICS

    Start with a song.
    Or a video.

    There’s no mode to choose. ClassTempo recognizes the file you open.

    1. Tap Open.

      It’s at the top of the player. Choose a song from Files, or a video from Photos or Files. Your phone may call the file option Choose File.

    2. Choose your file.

      It opens paused. ClassTempo also tries to save a copy in Library. If it cannot save, it tells you; keep the original.

    3. Tap Play.

      Tap Pause whenever you need to stop. Tap or drag on the waveform—or the video’s seek line—to move to another point.

    4. Set the pace.

      Use the tempo slider or −1% and +1%. 80% is slower than the original; 100% is the original speed. Tap the 100% reset button to return to it.

    Choose your music source.

    Use Open for MP3, M4A, WAV or a video file. Use Library → Apple Music to stream a song from your subscription in the familiar player. Apple Music currently plays at its original speed. Subscription downloads cannot be imported as ordinary files, and Spotify is not connected.

    Your everyday controls

    Restart
    Return to the beginning. With an active practice, Section returns to its start. During a class, Again restarts and plays the exercise.
    Tempo
    For imported files, change speed from 50% to 150%. Audio keeps its musical pitch; video pitch preservation depends on the browser. Apple Music currently stays at 100%. BPM, when shown for a local song, is the app’s estimated beat rate.
    Talk
    For an imported audio file, lower the app’s music level to 20% while you speak. For video or Apple Music, pause playback. Tap Finish talking to restore the level or resume a source that was playing.
    Keep awake
    Ask the phone to keep its screen on while ClassTempo is visible. Tap again to allow sleep. If the browser does not support this control, it is not shown. It does not prevent calls or notifications.
    Stage
    Open a larger teaching view. Video gets the most space, with controls below it. Exit returns to the regular player.

    02 · YOUR LIBRARY

    Keep everything
    within reach.

    Open is the fastest way to choose a new file. Library is where you return to what you’ve saved.

    1. Tap Library, then Media.

      Songs and videos appear together, with a Song or Video label. Recent files appear first.

    2. Tap a title to open it.

      The player is ready and paused. Tap Play when you want to begin.

    3. Use Add media to bring in more.

      Select one or several files. All selected files are saved when storage succeeds, and the first opens in the player.

    Search looks inside your library.

    Type part of a saved filename. Results update as you type. This field does not search Apple Music, Spotify or the internet.

    The × beside a file removes its saved copy from ClassTempo. Your original file stays where it was. Classes can keep a missing-file entry so you can import the original again.

    Play from Apple Music

    1. Open Library, then Apple Music.

      Connect using the Apple account with your music subscription. Sign in on Apple’s page; ClassTempo does not see your password.

    2. Find a song.

      Search by song or artist, or open My songs or Playlists. These lists currently show the first 25 items.

    3. Select it, then tap Play.

      The picker closes and the song opens paused in the familiar ClassTempo player. Use Play/Pause, Restart, the seek line and Stage.

    4. Mark a moment with a cue.

      Tap an empty cue to save the current position. A saved Apple Music cue moves the song there and pauses; tap Play to continue. Edit cues lets you move, rename or clear a bookmark.

    Streaming stays online and at normal speed.

    Apple Music needs internet and an active subscription. Use imported files for tempo changes, named practices, class plans and stems. Streaming songs are not copied into your saved Media or available offline through ClassTempo.

    Use Open to choose a local file directly, or Library → Files & classes to return to your saved files. Apple Music cue bookmarks stay in this browser; they are not included in the local-file settings backup.

    03 · CUES

    Find that moment.
    Save it with one tap.

    Use cues for a chorus, a musical break, or the phrase you keep teaching.

    1. Find the position you want.

      Play to it or move along the waveform. Select the Cues pill if Stems is showing.

    2. Tap an empty pad.

      + Set cue becomes a saved cue at the current position. You can do this while playing or paused.

    3. Tap the saved cue to play from it.

      Each file has eight cue slots. Reopening the same file restores its saved cues on this device.

    Give cues useful names

    Tap Edit cues. Use the pencil to rename a cue, × to clear it, or tap its pad to move it to your current position. Tap Done when finished. Undo reverses recent cue changes.

    TRY THIS

    Name your first three cues Intro, Basic step and Chorus. Labels are easier to recognize during class than timestamps.

    A cue inside the active practice range keeps the practice going. A cue outside it switches to full-track playback and shows a message.

    04 · PRACTICE

    Teach one phrase.
    As many times as you need.

    Save a named practice with its range, speed and repeat settings. You can reuse it later or add it to a class.

    1. Tap Practice.

      Playback pauses so you can set up the exercise. Enter a Practice name, such as “Basic step slowly.”

    2. Set the start and end.

      Use Start at cue and End at cue, enter times in seconds, or use Set start here and Set end here. The end must come after the start.

    3. Choose the pace and repetitions.

      Choose Repeat or Once. For Repeat, scroll inside the panel to Total plays and Pause between (seconds). Total plays includes the first play; choose Until stopped to keep repeating. Set the speed under Teaching tempo.

    4. Tap Start practice.

      This saves your setup and starts it. Use Save practice to save without starting. The player shows the practice name and pass progress.

    A SIMPLE FIRST PRACTICE

    Select a short phrase. Choose Repeat, set Total plays to 3 plays, Pause between to 2 seconds and Teaching tempo to 90%. Students hear the phrase three times, with a short reset between each pass.

    ■

    Finished practicing?

    Tap End practice. It pauses at your current position and returns to normal Play. Then tap Play to continue the full file at the current tempo. Your named practices are still saved.

    Delay, gaps and saved practices
    • Start delay: wait a few seconds before the exercise begins so you can move into position. It is a timer, not a spoken or beat-matched count-in.
    • Pause between (seconds): leave a gap between repeat passes. A pass is one play through the selected range.
    • Saved practices: reopen a named exercise for this file. Use + New practice to create another. You can save up to 12 practices per file.
    • Pause: stop the current run and cancel pending delays or gaps. Use End practice when you want ordinary full-track playback.
    • Close (×): discard changes you have not saved.
    • Video’s first start: the browser may require an immediate Play tap to enable sound. A start delay applies on later starts.

    05 · CLASSES

    Prepare once.
    Stay with your students.

    A class brings your files and practices into an ordered lesson. Once you start teaching, the next exercise stays right in the player.

    Build your teaching order

    1. Open Library → Classes.

      Create a class and give it a name, such as “Tuesday beginners.” For an existing class, choose Edit class.

    2. Add full files or saved practices.

      Use Add a full song or video for the whole file. Use Add a saved practice for a named exercise, even if its song is not currently open.

    3. Arrange the lesson.

      Use the up/down arrows to reorder. Tap an exercise to give it a friendly name or a teaching note. You can add the same file more than once.

    4. Tap Save class, then Teach class.

      ClassTempo checks that the saved files are available. The first exercise opens paused. Tap Play when the class is ready.

    Choose the right kind of entry.

    A full file starts at 100% with no repeat or delay. A saved practice brings its own range, tempo, repetitions, gap, delay and video settings.

    Teach from the player

    Next & play
    Open the next exercise and start it, using its prepared settings.
    Again
    Restart and play this exercise with its prepared range and repeat settings. Your current tempo stays; the start delay is skipped.
    Running order
    Tap the class strip above the file title. Select any exercise, or Previous, to open it paused. Notes appear in this list.
    Exit class
    Leave the lesson and stop playback. Resume returns to your saved place paused. The class itself is not deleted.
    Finish class
    Mark the session complete when you reach the final exercise. Your saved class is ready to use another day.
    Do the files play one after another?

    Only when Auto-next is on. It is off by default so you decide when to move on. Turn it on in the running order, or choose automatic continuation in Edit class.

    A full file advances when it ends. A finite practice advances after its last pass. Repeat until stopped never advances on its own—use Next & play. The last exercise finishes without looping back to the first.

    See the running order and Exit class

    Tap the class strip in the player to open this list. Choose an exercise to open it paused, turn Auto-next on or off, or use Exit class to leave the lesson.

    Change an exercise without changing everything else

    Each class entry keeps its own copy of the prepared settings. Changing the original saved practice later does not update an entry already added to a class. Adjusting tempo during teaching does not silently rewrite the class plan. To replace an entry’s setup, open the matching file and configure the player, then use Edit class → exercise details → Use current player settings → Save class.

    Cues belong to the file and are shared wherever that file is used. Stem selections are not saved with class exercises. If you remove an exercise or change a class after exiting, the app uses the saved class when you resume; an entry that no longer exists cannot be resumed.

    Resume restores the exercise and your position, paused. It does not continue an unfinished countdown or remember how many practice passes you had completed.

    06 · VIDEO & STAGE

    Give the movement
    the whole stage.

    Use the regular player to set things up. Tap Stage when you want the class to see the video.

    1. Open a video and tap Stage.

      The picture becomes larger, with playback and the seek line below it. Turn your phone sideways when that suits the video.

    2. Open only the controls you need.

      Cues reveals a small numbered strip below the picture. Swipe it horizontally to reach all eight slots. Tap an empty number to save a cue or a saved number to play it.

    3. Use Tools for adjustments.

      Change tempo, Restart, Talk, Mirror or Fit/zoom. Mirror flips the picture left to right. Fit shows the whole frame; zoom makes it larger and can crop the edges.

    4. Close a tray to give space back to the video.

      Class running order also sits below the picture. End practice remains available when a practice is active. Tap Exit to return to the regular player.

    Exit leaves Stage. Exit class leaves a lesson. End practice leaves a repeat. These are separate actions.

    07 · STEMS

    Help students
    hear the rhythm.

    Stems separates an audio file into parts so you can emphasize what students should listen for.

    1. Open a song and select Stems.

      Use the pill beside Cues. Stems works with audio files, not videos.

    2. Tap Prepare stems.

      The first preparation downloads about 80 MB of models plus the runtime. Use Wi-Fi and keep ClassTempo visible until preparation finishes. Your song stays on the device.

    3. Tap Use stems.

      Tap Vocal, Instruments, Bass or Drums to mute or hear that part. The On/Off labels show what is audible.

    4. Return to the recording with Original.

      Playback stays at the current position. Successfully saved stems can reopen without preparing the same song again.

    Before using stems

    Preparing and playing stems use extra memory. Low-memory devices may stop playback or close ClassTempo. Prepare before class and close unused apps.

    4:30Maximum song length for preparing stems.
    Also limited to 100 MB per source file.

    This limit applies only to stems preparation. Ordinary song and video playback has no duration cap.

    Acapella
    Hear the vocal part.
    Instrumental
    Hear the separated accompaniment without the vocal part.
    All
    Hear all four separated parts together.
    Low / High drums
    Focus on low or high drum frequencies. These are filters, not separately isolated kick and hi-hat recordings.

    Separation can leave audible artifacts. Use Original when you want the untouched recording. Saved stems & details lets you inspect preparation information or remove the current song’s saved stems.

    08 · READY FOR CLASS

    A little preparation.
    A smoother lesson.

    Add it to your Home Screen

    1. Open ClassTempo in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Open Share; depending on your Safari layout, it may be inside the page menu.
    3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If Open as Web App appears, leave it on. Tap Add.
    4. Launch the new icon once while online, then import your class media there.

    Apple’s illustrated setup steps ↗

    On Android, open ClassTempo in Chrome and use Install app or Add to Home screen when offered.

    Before students arrive

    • Connect your speaker in the phone’s settings and play a short test.
    • Check phone and speaker volume.
    • Open your class and check that its files are available.
    • Try a song, a video and the repeat you plan to teach.
    • Use Keep awake if you need the display visible.
    • If you need offline playback, test your saved files without a connection before class.

    Keep your files and settings safe

    Your library lives in this browser or Home Screen installation on this device. There is no account or cloud sync. Use the same installation to prepare and teach; Safari and the installed app may have separate storage.

    1. Open Library → Backup & device storage.

      Tap Export settings and keep the downloaded backup somewhere safe.

    2. Keep your original songs and videos separately.

      The settings backup contains classes, cues and practice settings. It does not contain media files or prepared stems.

    3. To restore, choose Restore settings.

      Select your backup, import the missing original media, and reopen the current track. Restore adds missing items and keeps existing settings.

    Offline means saved on this device.

    After an online launch caches the app, successfully saved media can play without a connection while the files and app cache remain available. Clearing site data or browser storage cleanup can remove them. Keep originals and a separate settings backup.

    Updates, lock-screen controls and interruptions

    Updates: open the app online. Close and reopen it to use a downloaded update. ClassTempo does not reload an ongoing class automatically.

    Lock-screen or headphone controls: start playback in the app first. Availability varies by device. Previous/Next controls navigate cues or restart a practice section; they do not select the next class exercise. Use Next & play in the app for that.

    Interruptions: calls, voice assistants and other apps may stop playback. Return to ClassTempo and use Tap to resume audio if shown, then Play. Test background repetition and class progression on your own phone before relying on them during a lesson.

    09 · QUICK ANSWERS

    Back to teaching.

    Start with the symptom you see.

    There’s no sound.

    Tap Play in ClassTempo. Check the phone’s volume and the speaker connection. If Finish talking is showing, tap it to restore the music level. If you are using stems, choose All or Original. After an interruption, tap Tap to resume audio if it appears.

    Search doesn’t find my song.

    Media search finds filenames already saved on this device. Use Open or Add media for another file. To search the Apple Music catalog, open Library → Apple Music and use its Search tab. It searches song and artist names, not your saved filenames.

    I opened a video while looking at songs. Where did it go?

    There is one Media library for both. A successful import opens its first selected file in the player and saves it with a Song or Video label. Use Library → Media to find it again. If saving failed, keep the original and try importing it again when storage is available.

    The practice keeps repeating—or I want the whole song again.

    Tap End practice, then Play. Pause alone stops the current run but keeps the practice selected. To repeat a fixed number of times, open Practice and choose a finite repeat count instead of repeating until stopped.

    My practice doesn’t repeat.

    Open Practice. Check that the end is after the start, choose Repeat rather than playing once, and tap Start practice. If you jump to a cue outside the selected range, ClassTempo switches to full-track playback; start the practice again to restore the repeat.

    The next class exercise hasn’t started.

    Classes wait for you by default. Tap Next & play, or turn on Auto-next in the running order. An exercise set to repeat until stopped stays on that exercise even with Auto-next on. If the browser holds a video paused, tap Play. Missing files and playback errors need attention before you continue.

    A video is blank, freezes or won’t decode.

    Try Retry video. Make sure a cloud-stored file has finished downloading. If it still fails, try an exported 1080p MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio; large 4K files can be more demanding. A different export may be needed for your device. Use Copy details on the error screen when reporting the problem.

    Stems won’t prepare, or a separated part sounds rough.

    Use an audio file no longer than 4:30 and no larger than 100 MB. Stay on Wi-Fi for the first download and keep the app visible. Retry preparation if an error offers that option; copy its details if it continues. Separation is imperfect: switch to Original for the untouched song.

    A saved file or class is missing on another device.

    ClassTempo does not sync devices. Import a settings backup and the original media on the other device. If a class lists a missing file, import that original again. Prepared stems are not in the settings backup and may need to be prepared again.

    Can I use Apple Music or Spotify?

    Apple Music is available through Library with an active music subscription and internet connection. Choose a song, then use the familiar player at its original speed. Tempo changes, named practices, class plans and stems use imported files. Spotify is not connected. Subscription downloads are not ordinary files ClassTempo can import.
